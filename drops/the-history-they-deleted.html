The History They Deleted

PA schoolbooks call every non-Muslim ruler an “occupier” and every Muslim conquest a “liberation.” The older editions still mentioned the Jewish kingdoms. The current ones deleted them. Europe funds the system: €400 million — “not a single word” changed.

OCTOBER 13, 2026 · 6 MIN READ

A chart went viral this week. It lists every ruler of the land of Palestine — Canaanites, Egyptians, Israelites, Assyrians, Persians, Greeks, Romans, Byzantines, caliphs, Crusaders, Saladin, Mamluks, Ottomans, Britons, Israelis — and shows what Palestinian Authority schoolbooks call each of them. The pattern is perfect: ruled by a Muslim empire, the land was “liberated.” Ruled by anyone else, it was “occupied.” And the only independent states ever ruled from inside the land itself — the kingdoms of Israel and Judah, the Hasmoneans — are not mentioned at all.

We checked the claims behind the chart. The core of it holds — and one detail is worse than the chart says.

I. The chart

The infographic, by @MaxNordau, works through the PA's Social Studies textbooks for grades 6 to 8, citing chapter and page. Egypt: “occupation.” Assyria, Babylon, Persia, Greece, Rome, Byzantium: “occupation.” The Crusaders: “occupied it.” Britain: “colonialism.” Israel: “Zionist occupation” — throughout. But the Arab caliphates that conquered the land in 636: “liberation.” Saladin, a Kurd who ruled from Cairo and Damascus: “liberation.” The Turkic Mamluks: merely “ruled.” The Ottoman Turks, who ran the place from Istanbul for 400 years: “Ottoman rule” — and, according to the chart's reading, they don't even appear on the textbook's list of Palestine's invaders.

The rule the chart proposes is simple: Muslim, not an occupier. Conquest, if done in the name of Islam, is liberation.

II. The verification

We verified what we could against the independent watchdogs that have audited these books for years — IMPACT-se, Palestinian Media Watch, and the researchers David Bedein and Arnon Groiss. The pattern the chart describes is real:

Israel is, in IMPACT-se's words, “almost always referred to as the 'Zionist Occupation.'” A Grade 6 assignment — documented by Bedein — asks students to compare “the Roman occupation of Carthage” with “the Zionist occupation of Palestine,” in the same breath. Saladin-as-liberation is confirmed as the curriculum's model: IMPACT-se documents a “strategy of revolution modeled after Saladin's victory over the Crusades,” with terror deemed legitimate “in the case of liberation movements.”

And the erasure of Jewish history is total in the current books. IMPACT-se's 2025–26 review of 290 textbooks: “any acknowledgement of Jewish history is absent.” Groiss: “complete denial of Jewish history in the land.” One textbook skips Jerusalem's history from the Jebusites straight to Roman Aelia — deleting a thousand years.

Here is the detail worse than the chart: the kingdoms were mentioned — once. A Hebrew University study of the older, 2014 edition found its timetable listing “David's Kingdom 1000–923 BC; Northern Kingdom of Israel 923–722 BC; Kingdom of Judea 923–586 BC” — and notes that this “no longer exists in the present books.” They didn't forget the Jewish kingdoms. They removed them.

Honesty requires the caveats: the chart's exact page citations and the invaders-list claim rest on the infographic alone — we could not independently check every page — and one PA-adjacent document does list the Ottomans among past occupying powers, so the Ottoman point should stay scoped to the cited textbook pages. The core thesis, though, is documented six ways over.

III. The money

These books are not some fringe pamphlet. They are published by the “State of Palestine, Ministry of Education,” mandatory in every school in the West Bank and Gaza — including UNRWA schools — reaching more than 1.3 million children. And they are, indirectly, European-funded: EU money flows to the PA education sector through the PEGASE mechanism, paying the salaries of the system that writes and teaches this curriculum.

Europe knows. The European Parliament has condemned the textbooks seven years running. In May 2026 it voted 418 to 207 to make future funding conditional on removing antisemitic content, incitement, and the glorification of jihad and martyrdom. Under a July 2024 Letter of Intent, over €400 million was transferred on the PA's promise of curriculum reform. Then, that September, a PA Education Ministry official said it out loud: not a single word has been deleted or altered in the textbooks.

Four hundred million euros. Not a single word.

IV. The meaning

Groiss, who has studied these books for 25 years, summarizes the curriculum's three principles: opposition to Israel's existence anywhere, demonization of Jews, and a call for violent struggle for total “liberation.” Grade 10 students learn that jihad is a private obligation for every Muslim — necessary “for the liberation of Palestine.”

Now re-read the chart with that in mind. “Liberation” is not a description of the past. It is an instruction for the future. The history lesson — Muslim conquest good, all else occupation, Jewish sovereignty unmentionable — is the moral license for the ideology lesson: the land must be “liberated” again, the way Saladin did it.

A child taught this history does not need to be recruited. She has been graduated.

The eraser

The most important word in this story is not “occupation” or “liberation.” It is deleted. Somewhere in Ramallah, an editor opened the history book, found David's kingdom in the timetable, and took it out. That is not bias drifting in with the culture. That is a decision, made by a ministry, funded by Europe, taught to a million children.

History is written by the victors, they say. In Ramallah, it is deleted by the curriculum committee.
