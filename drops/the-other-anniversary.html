The Other Anniversary

On the morning of October 7th, Turkey's state broadcaster aired a highlight reel of Hamas's attack — Quranic verses about victory laid over footage of the breached Gaza fence. No murders. No victims. One day earlier, in Paris, three names were carved into stone.

OCTOBER 7, 2026 · 4 MIN READ

On the morning of October 7th — one day after Paris carved three names into stone — Turkey's state broadcaster aired a highlight reel of the morning those names became history.

The video is 51 seconds long. It carries the watermark of TRT Arabi, the Arabic-language arm of TRT, Turkey's state broadcaster. Its footage is labeled, in Arabic, “Gaza envelope” and dated 2023-10-07. Over images of the breached border fence, verses from the Quran celebrate victory — rendered in the clip's subtitles as “we will come to them with armies they cannot withstand.”

I. The broadcast

The clip surfaced on Tuesday morning via Abu Ali Express, the widely-followed Israeli Telegram channel covering Arab affairs, which posted it at 11:05 with a note that has the shape of a verdict: the video supports Hamas's terror attack of that morning, it wrote, presenting “highlights” of Hamas euphoria — with no mention whatsoever of the acts of murder, and no reference to the victims.

Watch the 51 seconds yourself and the description holds. There are no victims in the reel. No names, no faces, no aftermath. There is the fence, the date, the verses — and euphoria, presented as a highlight package.

II. The channel

TRT Arabi is not a fringe outlet. It is the Arabic service of TRT, the national public broadcaster of Turkey — a NATO member state — and, as Abu Ali Express put it, “a legitimate media outlet in the West.” Its correspondents hold press credentials; its studios sit in Istanbul; its signal is state-funded.

Its homepage on the anniversary tells you how the channel frames the day: coverage filed under the banner of “Tufan al-Aqsa” — the Al-Aqsa Flood — which is Hamas's own name for the October 7th attack. The broadcaster did not merely report the anniversary. It adopted the attacker's title for it.

This does not come from nowhere. Turkey's president has praised Hamas as a “liberation group” of mujahideen and stated plainly that Hamas is not a terrorist organization. The broadcast is the state television of that position, doing what state television does: turning policy into pictures.

III. The contrast

Twenty-four hours earlier, in Paris, the mayor inaugurated the Allée Ariel, Kfir et Shiri Bibas in the Parc Monceau — the first permanent tribute to the Bibas family anywhere in European public space. A mother and two small boys, murdered in captivity, given a path in a park where children will walk for a century.

Two commemorations of the same morning. One carved the victims' names into stone. The other aired victory verses over the fence they were dragged through.

The choice

Memory is a choice, and broadcasters make it in public. Paris chose stone: three names, a path, a century of strangers asking who they were. Ankara's state television chose a highlight reel: the fence, the date, the verses — and not a single victim.

Three years on, the world is still deciding what October 7th was. Watch what the commemorations commemorate, and you will know exactly where everyone stands.
